> Read the fourth column against the second and the whole instruction is 1.45 mm on a 60 mm brow. Read the fifth column against the second and the width of everything anyone has ever called acceptable for this face is 3.74 mm, or 0.15 in. The two angle columns at the edges are ±1 SD around the only brow angle ever measured, and that measurement recorded no face shape, so the edges are this page's arithmetic rather than anyone's finding. Conversions are exact division by 25.4. Lengths are measured along the brow, not straight across, which is why a 60 mm brow spans rather less than 60 mm of face.

## Brows for a square face, in the order the advice was given

*Every dated position on this face's brows, oldest first. Nine rows, twelve years and four months, and the fourth column is the point: none of them measured.*

| 10 Nov 2013 (machine-readable date on the page reads 25 Dec 2023) | A salon's blog, part of a series on face shapes | The brow must produce some sort of elongation; a set of well arched brows achieves balance. | 15 Feb 2021 (page prints an update of 13 Oct 2022) | A lash and brow studio's guide | A higher, more curved arch softens and elongates, offsetting the harder angles of the jaw. States the relation this page's band depends on: the rounder the face, the more acute the angle the brows can carry. | No. Lists three public figures, only one of them shared with the 2020 list. |

| 13 Jan 2023 (page prints an update of 23 Apr 2023) | A lash brand's face-shape series | Four named styles for this face: a tail-arched brow with the arch starting closer to the tail than the centre; a softly rounded brow, but not a full semi-circle, which backfires; a medium-high arch if the face is short in its lower two thirds; and a flat brow tilted upward, with no arch at all, if the forehead is low. | 8 Nov 2024 (page prints 15 Nov 2024) | A brow-product brand's guide | Three shapes offered at once: a softly arched brow with a subtle arch tapering to the tail; a rounded brow, for youth and to counterbalance the strong lines; and an angled brow with a defined peak, which elongates, provided it is not too harsh. | A landmark for the arch — above the outer edge of the iris, which is not the point the 2021 row gives. Keep the brow soft but defined so it complements the bone structure without looking overly sculpted; the goal is to soften the strong angles, and a gentle arch counterbalances the angular jaw. Shape a lifted arch with the tail tapering naturally, and keep the brow full. Brows should follow bone structure and natural growth patterns, not trends. > Nine rows, 10 November 2013 to 23 March 2026 — 4,516 days, or twelve years, four months and thirteen days. Where a source prints a date that differs from its machine-readable one, both are given and the printed one decides the order; that is the first row, whose own text supplies an original date a decade before the one in its markup. Four sources were retrieved and excluded rather than used to pad the count: one carries no date of any kind, one returned an access-error page instead of its article, one returned a 403, and one is an image collection with no publication date. Sources are described here by what they are and never named or linked, and the fourth column records only what each source printed itself — never this page's estimate.
