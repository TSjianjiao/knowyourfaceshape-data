## How do you measure the shape — millimetres or inches?

*The peak band for three brow lengths, counted in from the inner start — the same method as the worked example above*

| Brow length | In inches | Peak band from inner start | Peak band in inches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 52 mm | 2.05 in | 34.7 – 39.0 mm | 1.37 – 1.54 in |
| 58 mm | 2.28 in | 38.7 – 43.5 mm | 1.52 – 1.71 in |
| 64 mm | 2.52 in | 42.7 – 48.0 mm | 1.68 – 1.89 in |

> The peak band runs from brow length minus one third of brow length to brow length minus one quarter of it — two thirds to three quarters of the total, measured from the inner start. Every inch figure is the millimetre figure divided by 25.4.

## Round-face brow advice, by the date it was published

*Every published position on round-face brows this page could date, from 28 February 2023 to 14 September 2026*

| Published | Source | What it published as a number or a measurable instruction | What it told a round face to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 28 Feb 2023 | Brow studio, founder-authored face-shape guide | No measurement for the brow itself. Defines the round face as equally long from forehead to chin and from ear to ear. | “The best brow for a round face is one with an arch” — but soft and angled only, because “a rounded brow shape will just round out the face even more.” |
| 7 Jul 2025 (updated 9 Jul 2026) | National brow-studio chain, face-type guide | Names threading as the best service for achieving one. “Fuller brows often suit round or heart-shaped faces, while softer, medium-thickness brows flatter oval or long faces.” | “Eyebrow shape for a round face: try a high arch.” |
| 16 Jul 2025 | Waxing chain, pro-tips page | Map = three points (lowest, highest, tail), the start line drawn “straight upward from the sides of your nostrils.” Shape holds two to three weeks. | “A lifted and angled arch helps to lengthen the appearance of round faces.” Concedes there are “no hard-and-fast rules.” |
| 23 Jan 2026 | Brow-focused beauty editorial site | Peak “slightly higher than your baseline.” “Avoid over-plucking the front of your brow — you need that density for balance on round features.” For sparse brows, a growth serum applied for two to three months. | “A pronounced arch is non-negotiable for round face shapes.” Blueprint: pronounced arch, extended tapered tail, full front definition. |
| 4 Jul 2026 | Salon blog, signed by a named staff member | Placement “slightly farther out for extra lift.” “A slightly longer brow with a tapered tail can also flatter a round face by visually stretching your features.” Warns that “a barely-there arch placed too close to the inner eye does not give you the lift a round face needs.” | “High, angled brows with a clear arch are widely considered the best.” But also “not every round face has to wear the exact same brow.” |
| 14 Sep 2026 | National beauty magazine, quoting a cosmetics brand's national brow authority | No measurement for this face. | Curved, round brows are assigned to heart-shaped faces — not to round ones. “A more horizontal brow (or a brow with less arch) will provide width and overall balance to a longer face shape.” |
