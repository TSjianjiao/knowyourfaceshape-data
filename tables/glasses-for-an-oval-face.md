# Glasses for an Oval Face: Five Frame Parameters, Three Outlines

Live page: <https://knowyourfaceshape.com/blog/glasses-for-an-oval-face/>

## The five rows, three outlines across

*Rows are the five parameters that decide whether a frame can be worn, made and ordered. Columns are three of this site's outlines, chosen because they are the three whose eyewear advice is published in English at a length worth comparing; the other four are not printed here because no English fitting page attaches a millimetre to them, and a blank column would say more about the research than about your face. Every cell is a published band restated in millimetres with inches alongside, except the square column's depth, which is arithmetic. Ratios and units: none of these numbers is a proportion, and none of them moves if you change your outline and keep your width.*

| Frame parameter | Oval | Round | Square |
|---|---|---|---|

| Lens width, first stamped number | 50 – 56 mm (1.97 – 2.20 in) | 50 – 56 mm (1.97 – 2.20 in) | 46 – 53 mm (1.81 – 2.09 in) |
| Lens depth, rarely stamped | 32 – 42 mm (1.26 – 1.65 in) | 32 – 40 mm (1.26 – 1.57 in) | 46 – 53 mm (1.81 – 2.09 in), a round lens being as deep as it is wide |

Lens depth is not on this ladder, because depth follows the style you chose and the prescription you are wearing.*
