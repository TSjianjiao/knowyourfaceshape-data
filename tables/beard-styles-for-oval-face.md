# Beard Styles for an Oval Face: The Two Numbers Nobody Publishes

Live page: <https://knowyourfaceshape.com/blog/beard-styles-for-oval-face/>

## The length chart

*Beard lengths for an oval face — set the dial or the guard to the third column*

| Where it goes | Tool | Length | What that number buys you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sideburns | Clipper, one pass | 1 – 3 mm · 0.039 – 0.118 in | Transition zone. Short enough that the beard does not tie into the haircut, long enough that the edge reads as deliberate. One pass at 1 mm or 3 mm and it is done evenly — sideburn hair stands stiff enough for a guard to hold it. |
| Upper cheek | Trimmer dial | 3 – 6 mm · 0.118 – 0.236 in | The band that reads as maintained rather than as day two. Below 3 mm on this zone the growth looks like a patch that failed to fill; above 6 mm it starts adding width at a point where the face does not need it. |
| Lower cheek / jaw line | Trimmer dial | 5 – 10 mm · 0.197 – 0.394 in (#2 – #3) | The zone that fills the jaw’s natural taper. This is where an oval face can carry more than a round one — the jaw is already narrower than the cheekbones, so length here follows the bone instead of redrawing it. Ten millimetres is the ceiling, not the target. |
| Chin | Trimmer dial or guard | 3 – 10 mm · 0.118 – 0.394 in (#1 – #3) | Held equal to or shorter than the cheeks. The moment the chin runs longer than the jaw, the face starts extending downward — which is the one direction this outline cannot afford. A rounded or square chin line at 5 to 10 mm follows the jaw. A pointed chin at the same length extends it. The shape of the edge matters as much as the number. |
| Chin — the line you do NOT cross | — | >10 mm · >0.394 in | Past ten millimetres the chin beard stops following the outline and starts redrawing it downward. Ducktails, extended goatees and Van Dykes live here — and those are round-face styles, built to add length to a face that has none. On an oval face they add length to a face that already has it, and the ratio moves toward oblong. |
| Moustache | Trimmer dial | 3 – 6 mm · 0.118 – 0.236 in | Level with the chin zone or shorter. Trim everything above the lip line flat; press the comb down and take the overhang off in one pass. A moustache dressed downward at the corners drags the visual centre of the face with it. |
| Full-beard even — the default | Dial, one setting | 3 – 5 mm · 0.118 – 0.197 in | The easiest choice and the one most growers land on. One number all the way round, no zone differential, follows the outline instead of redrawing it. Three to five millimetres reads as a beard rather than as stubble and rather than as a shape — on this outline, that neutrality is the point. |

> Guard fractions are the sizes Wahl stamps on its combs: #1 = 1/8 in (3 mm), #2 = 1/4 in (6 mm), #3 = 3/8 in (10 mm on Wahl’s European chart, 9.5 mm on its American one — the discrepancy is real and is addressed below). Decimal inches are conversions at 25.4 mm to the inch. Rows under 1.6 mm have no guard; they are dial settings.
