# Pear Shaped Face: One Column, Five Bands, and the Square Boundary

## The table: five checks, two columns

*Bands are the published centre plus or minus one standard deviation, read on the tape scale. Ratios carry no units; the jaw angle is in degrees. This site counts pear inside the triangle column and lists no eighth shape — guides that split this family into base-up and base-down are read the same way, with the base-down half landing here.*

| Check | Triangle (this label) | Square |
|---|---|---|
| Face length ÷ cheekbones | 1.12 – 1.48 | 0.87 – 1.23 |
| Forehead ÷ cheekbones | 0.72 – 0.88 | 0.87 – 1.03 |
| Jaw ÷ cheekbones | 0.96 – 1.20 | 0.83 – 1.07 |
| Jaw angle | 110 – 126° | 100 – 116° |
| Chin taper (chin width ÷ jaw width) | 0.41 – 0.58 | 0.47 – 0.63 |

> Four notes belong to the reading of this table rather than sitting under it as a disclaimer — the boundary section below depends on the first one.
