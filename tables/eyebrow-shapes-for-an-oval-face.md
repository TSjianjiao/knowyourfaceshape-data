## What is the best eyebrow shape for an oval face, in numbers?

*Face length at the two ratios that matter, for three cheekbone widths — the band between the last two columns is where the arch advice reverses*

| Cheekbone width | In inches | Face length at 1.50 | Face length at 1.65 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 130 mm | 5.12 in | 195 mm (7.68 in) | 214.5 mm (8.44 in) |
| 140 mm | 5.51 in | 210 mm (8.27 in) | 231 mm (9.09 in) |
| 150 mm | 5.91 in | 225 mm (8.86 in) | 247.5 mm (9.74 in) |

> Each length is the width multiplied by the ratio in that column. Every inch figure is the millimetre figure divided by 25.4 and rounded to two decimals. The published definition of this face shape is face length approximately 1.5 × face width; the word approximately is the source's own and no source checked gives a tolerance around it.

## Oval-face brow advice, by the date it was published

*Every published position on oval-face brows this page could date, from 25 December 2023 to 14 September 2026*

| Published | Source | What it published as a number or a measurable instruction | What it told an oval face to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 Dec 2023 | Threading salon blog, face-shape series | No measurement for the brow itself. States the governing principle as creating “the opposite effect of what the face naturally has.” | “A moderate arch. The beginning and ending of the brow should be on the same level.” A more pronounced arch elongates the face; a straight brow makes it “appear rounder and fatter.” |
| 7 Jul 2025 (updated 9 Jul 2026) | National brow-studio chain, face-type guide | Defines the shape as soft, rounded features with wide cheekbones. Thickness: “softer, medium-thickness brows flatter oval or long faces.” Service suggested for this face: a brow tint, for a subtle arch. | “Think soft angles.” While most brow styles can work, a soft arch maintains balance. “Avoid overly flat brows or a shape with sharp angles.” Calls the shape an “ideal” one, in quotation marks of its own. |
| 16 Jul 2025 (updated 28 Aug 2025) | Waxing chain, pro-tips page | Start point: “draw an imaginary line straight upward from the sides of your nostrils.” Three named points — lowest, highest, tail. Concedes there are “no hard-and-fast rules.” | “Soft angles with shallow arches suit the gentle features of oval faces best.” One sentence; no arch height, no tail rule, no thickness. |
| 2 Feb 2026 | Brow-focused editorial site | Start from a pencil held vertically at the inner corner of the eye; arch from the nostril through the pupil; tail from the nostril through the outer corner of the eye. Arch “about one-third higher than the inner brow.” Tail angle “about 10-15 degrees” downward. Thickness medium. | A soft arch, “the middle ground between completely straight and extremely angled.” Thin over-plucked brows make this face “look pinched”; extremely bold brows overwhelm it. A tail that is too long “can make your face appear wider than it is.” |
| 9 Aug 2026 | Regional waxing and threading chain, staff-reviewed guide | Face length “approximately one and a half times your face width.” Start aligned with the inner corner of the eye; peak above the outer edge of the iris; end on a line from the nostril through the outer corner of the eye. Maintenance every 3-4 weeks. | “For oval faces, the universally flattering choice is a soft angled arch.” Caution list: high dramatic arches (appear longer, read surprised or severe); overly thin brows (age the appearance, difficult to grow back); extremely thick straight brows (blocky). |
| 14 Sep 2026 | National beauty magazine, quoting a cosmetics brand's national brow authority and a celebrity brow stylist | No measurement for this face. Map given as three points, all run from the nose: start from the dimple, arch from the outer edge of the nose across the centre of the eye, end from the outside of the nose to the outer corner of the eye. Claim: “99.9 percent of faces will look complementary with a soft angled eyebrow” — the stylist's own figure, with no method given. | “Straight brows work best for elongated, oval face shapes.” “A more horizontal brow (or a brow with less arch) will provide width and overall balance to a longer face shape.” Soft angled brows work too, “then again, most face shapes can.” |

> One position per row, in publication-date order. Span covered: 25 December 2023 to 14 September 2026 — two years, eight months and 20 days.
